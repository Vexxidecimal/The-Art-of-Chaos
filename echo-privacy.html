Echo privacy policy

Echo keeps your information on your phone. Here is exactly what it touches.

Effective: November 5, 2026 · App: Echo (com.theartofchaos.echo) · Publisher: The Art Of Chaos

Echo is built to keep your information on your phone. This policy explains exactly what the app touches.

No account, no ads, no analytics. Echo has no sign-in, shows no ads, and contains no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools.

Your mods stay on your phone. The mods you build, their scripts, and anything they remember (like checked boxes) are stored only in Echo's private storage on your device. They are not backed up to the cloud or sent to us. Removing a mod or uninstalling Echo deletes them.

Weather (only if you turn it on). If you allow a mod to show weather and grant approximate location, Echo rounds your location to about 1 km and sends it to the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway, api.met.no) to get a forecast. MET Norway's own privacy terms apply to that request. Echo does not store your location and never uses precise or background location.

Email count (only if you turn it on). If you allow a mod to count new emails and turn on Notification access for Echo, Echo looks at notifications from email apps on your phone and keeps, in memory only, the number of them and up to three sender names. It never reads or stores message text and ignores notifications from all other apps. Turn this off any time in your phone's notification access settings.

Opening apps and links. When you tap a button in a mod, Echo may open your browser or another app (like your email app). What happens there is covered by that app's policy.

Children. Echo is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact. We'll update this page if anything changes. Questions: theartofchaosllc@gmail.com.
